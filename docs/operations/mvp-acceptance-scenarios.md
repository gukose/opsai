# MVP acceptance scenarios

InternalDemo scenarios cover the local boundaries without live providers: checkout/departure cleaning and inspection; smart flash interruption; minibar inventory and human-approved charge; damage/Vision provenance and review; deterministic STT fixture to structured intent; opaque guest session and inbound request; rule-based HIGH risk and service recovery; shift required-read acknowledgement; idempotent night audit counter; and quality-weighted XP/leaderboard.

External limitations: Apaleo room-ready and folio posting are unsupported, external STT is disabled, and real Meta WhatsApp is disabled. Tests must never call those systems.
